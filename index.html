Sunrise arc. Magnetic light.

The latest Figma gradient opens from black and deep blue into blue, aqua, green and a small pale-yellow accent. Several soft color fields form the sunrise arc. Fine tonal grain drifts gently inward above the gradient and below the luminous rays and breathing cursor lights. The hues stay near their original positions.

Move slowly to attract approaching light. After it passes the cursor, the field fans outward. Fast passes exert little pull; slowing down lets the attraction build gently. The light carries its motion through a change of direction, then settles slowly. Linger in one place to deepen the local pull; it fades more slowly after a longer hover.

The surrounding field stays anchored, with no accumulated cursor trails. Converge draws inward; Radiate reverses the flow.

Their pull gradually fades as they drift into the star. Pause holds the points in place; Restart clears them.
